## What moved and why

Wednesday's tech-led rally (S&P +0.58%, Nasdaq +1.05%) unwound sharply in the morning session as the AI-capex trade took another hit. Tesla slid ~3.5% despite beating on revenue and EPS after cutting its annual capex forecast; Meta fell ~3% on a revenue beat as investors balked at new non-voting shares to fund its AI buildout; and Lam Research dropped ~8% on a soft outlook, dragging SOXX down 3%+. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's comment that neocloud demand is "off the charts" but capital is scarce summed up the mood. On the macro side, oil jumped back above $99 as the NYT reported US/Israeli plans for renewed strikes on Iran after the midterms and Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia raised supply-disruption fears — yet bonds caught a safe-haven bid, pulling the 10Y down to ~5.05%. Bitcoin slipped to ~$81.7k alongside the risk-off move in crypto-linked stocks. With no Fed decision until October 28, today's test is whether Wednesday's tech gains survive the session.
