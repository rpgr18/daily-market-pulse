## What moved and why

Thursday's session was the market's AI-spending reckoning: the Nasdaq fell 2.60%, its worst day since June, and the S&P 500 lost 1.21% as investors decided the debt-funded AI buildout may never earn a return — Meta sank ~7% on heavy capex, the SOX semiconductor index dropped 5.6% (AMD −8%, Micron −6%, Nvidia −4%), while the Dow eked out +0.02% and the equal-weight S&P actually rose, showing the damage was narrowly concentrated in mega-cap tech. Bonds absorbed the safe-haven bid, sending the 10-year yield down 19 bps to 4.95%, its biggest one-day drop since March 2023, which took a chunk out of priced-in Fed tightening ahead of next week's FOMC meeting. Friday morning the pressure rotated from chips to crude: WTI is near $101.4 and Brent above $115 on reports of possible renewed US/Israeli strikes on Iran and Houthi attacks on Saudi energy sites, pulling Dow futures down ~1% while tech futures stabilized.
