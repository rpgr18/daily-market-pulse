## Take

Tuesday morning was a rotation, not a retreat: renewed AI-financing worries (CoreWeave's debt load, weak chip names, a rough Elice IPO in Korea) dragged the Nasdaq down ~0.7% and tech ~1.3%, while health care (+2.3%), utilities, and staples lifted the Dow ~0.7% on broad participation. The 10-year yield eased ~3 bp to 5.10% as weak housing data (September pending home sales at a 25-year low) reinforced bets the Fed holds rates next week.
