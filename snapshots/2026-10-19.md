**Markets open and rebounding.** Wall Street opened higher Monday as AI-related stocks bounced
back from Friday's sharp AI-capex selloff, while crude oil retreated after Washington waived
part of its Russia sanctions and big earnings (Meta, Tesla, Netflix) loomed this week.

## The take

Friday's 2.1% Nasdaq plunge — the index's worst weekly drop since June on AI debt and spending
worries — is reversing this morning, with chip stocks (AMD, Nvidia) leading the rebound and
the VIX collapsing ~10% back below its long-term average near 19. Oil is the other big mover:
WTI fell nearly 2% toward $99.50 after President Trump waived key Graham Act sanctions on
Russia, raising the prospect of more Russian fuel reaching world markets, though Middle East
shipping disruptions remain a supply risk. Bond markets stay on edge with the 10-year above
5% as September CPI (3.7%) keeps an October Fed rate hike on the table.
