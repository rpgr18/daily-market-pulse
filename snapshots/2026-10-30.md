## Take

The tech-led selloff deepened on the final trading day of October after Amazon and Intel reported after Thursday's close — Amazon down about 7% on a sales miss and Intel plunging roughly 30% on a weak outlook — reigniting worries about the payoff on Big Tech's AI spending, a day after Alphabet's earnings miss had already dragged the Nasdaq down 1.38%.
