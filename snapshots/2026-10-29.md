## Take

Stocks slipped Thursday morning with tech leading the decline, as Alphabet fell roughly 12–13% after its Q3 results missed expectations and showed negative free cash flow of about $9.1 billion, reigniting worries about the payoff from heavy AI spending — while Microsoft rose on 48% Azure growth, and ServiceNow and Fortinet also dropped sharply on their own results. Amazon and Intel report after the close tonight.
