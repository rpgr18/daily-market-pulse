## AI & Tech

- **$2.4B in compute pledges for federal AI research** — Trump announced commitments from Nvidia ($1B), AMD ($500M), OpenAI ($200M), and Anthropic/Google ($150M each) to power the Genesis Mission across 14 agencies, while doubling down on rebranding AI as "super intelligence." ([NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/10/08/business/trump-unveils-2-4b-investment-from-tech-giants-including-nvidia-openai-to-fund-federal-ai-mission/))
- **Science medals for tech CEOs** — Trump awarded the National Medal of Science to Elon Musk ("modern day Thomas Edison"), Sergey Brin, Jensen Huang and Lisa Su, and the National Medal of Technology and Innovation to Michael Dell and Satya Nadella. ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/10/08/trump-elon-musk-tech-leaders-science-awards/92151187007/))
- **Lawmakers object to Google buying Spirit Airlines' data** — 120+ lawmakers led by Elizabeth Warren and Steven Horsford pushed back on Google's $10M purchase of the defunct airline's internal records (100M emails, 500M Teams messages) to train AI models, asking employee data be excluded. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/lawmakers-raise-alarm-google-plan-acquire-spirit-airlines-data-ai-models-2026-10-08/))
- **Microsoft cut from key visa program** — The Trump administration barred Microsoft from the PERM green-card program over fraud accusations from VP Vance, and suspended Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Capgemini and Adobe as well. ([NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/10/08/us-news/trump-admin-cuts-off-microsoft-from-major-visa-program-accuses-tech-giant-of-fraud-and-abuse/))
- **Model launch flurry** — OpenAI's GPT-6 introduces an "Intelligent UI" that turns prompts into interactive tools, Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5 for high-volume tasks, and Google rolled out its SynthID Detector globally for AI-generated media. ([Gadgets360](https://www.gadgets360.com/ai/news?from=gha))
- **The infrastructure race accelerates** — TSMC's September revenue jumped 54.6%, GlobalFoundries signed a five-year deal to manufacture advanced chip-packaging components for TSMC in New York, and Manus raised $500M+ after its proposed Meta acquisition unraveled. ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/08/top-tech-news-today-october-8-2026-globalfoundries-google-manus-microsoft-nvidia-openai-tencent-more/))
- **School board votes to ban AI for all students** — Frederick County, MD voted 6-1 to ban AI use from pre-K through 12th grade, one of the strictest student tech policies in the region, citing cheating and critical-thinking concerns. ([Fox 5 DC](https://fox5dc.com/news/frederick-county-school-board-votes-ban-ai-use-pre-k-through-12th-grade-students))
