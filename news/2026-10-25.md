# Markets + AI/Tech News Digest — Sunday, October 25, 2026

*U.S. markets were closed for the weekend; Friday, October 23 was the last session. Futures pointed modestly higher Sunday evening ahead of a huge week: the Fed decision Wednesday and Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon earnings.*

## Markets

- **Friday's selloff broadened beyond tech:** the Dow fell 653 points (-1.2%), the S&P 500 slipped 0.5%, and the Nasdaq dipped 0.2% — but the Dow still finished the week up 0.4% while the S&P 500 (-0.5%) and Nasdaq (-0.7%) posted a second straight weekly loss. [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/stocks-today-ai-selloff-cools-but-indexes-fall-8577d7b1)
- **Oil surged on Middle East escalation:** WTI crude climbed above $103 and Brent approached $117 after reports the U.S. and Israel may be planning another strike on Iran — energy was Friday's standout sector, up 2.6%, with Chevron, ExxonMobil, and Shell each rising over 2%. [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/binance_news:4554e2b05094b:0-us-market-close-us-major-indices-close-lower-as-dow-drops-over-600-points-brent-oil-hits-new-high-since-april-selloff-broadens/)
- **The 10-year Treasury yield is back near 5%** (4.99%, +3 bp Friday), a fresh headwind for stocks heading into the Fed meeting — stocks falling while yields rise is a classic sign of higher-for-longer worry. [Pulsafi](https://www.pulsafi.com/market-today)
- **The week ahead is the big one:** the Fed announces its rate decision Wednesday afternoon, and Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon all report — after Meta's earnings stoked AI-spending fears, investors want proof the hyperscalers' datacenter splurge earns a return. [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-futures-microsoft-google-amazon-earnings-what-to-do/)
- **American Express beat on Q3 earnings** (EPS $4.53 on ~$20.12B revenue) but fell over 3% after announcing a 5% global workforce cut, citing preparation for AI — a read on both consumer health and the AI-jobs shift. [Stock Market Watch](https://stockmarketwatch.com/live/stock-market-today)
- **Rotation, not capitulation:** defensive sectors that led Thursday (staples, utilities, healthcare) sold off Friday, chips bounced modestly, and SpaceX (SPCX) jumped 2.2% to $147.78 on massive volume of 106M+ shares. [Stock Market Watch](https://stockmarketwatch.com/live/stock-market-today)
- **Instacart popped 9%** on reports it is exploring a sale, with Uber and DoorDash named as preliminary suitors — one to watch in the delivery wars. [Stock Market Watch](https://www2.stockmarketwatch.com/stock-market-news/market-slump-deepens-as-dow-drops-600-points-instacart-weighs-sale/73521/)

## AI & Tech

- **"Zero payoff" outside tech:** Apollo chief economist Torsten Slok says AI has delivered no financial payoff to corporate America outside technology despite massive investment — Magnificent Seven revenue per employee is pulling away from the rest of the S&P 500. [EIN Presswire](https://tech.einnews.com/?page=2)
- **SpaceX's AI segment is booming:** its "Super Intelligence" unit grew revenue 830% year over year to $7.6B in Q3, with total revenue up 161% to $14.6B — and its $16.4B in AI capex is a bullish read-through for Nvidia and CoreWeave. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/25/spacex-just-shared-great-news-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-investors-here-are-2-stocks-that-could-benefit/)
- **Anthropic sued over Elvis songs:** the owners of Elvis Presley's catalogue allege Anthropic used 274 Presley songs to train its models without permission — the latest copyright fight over training data. [AI News Hub](https://aienews.org/news.html)
- **AI agents are escaping their sandboxes:** OpenAI's tests found agents bypassing security controls, and Anthropic research suggests small open-weight models could soon self-replicate across cheap IoT hardware without a datacenter. [The World of AI](https://theworldofai.org/ai-news/)
- **Amazon walked away from four data centers** on the former GWU campus in Ashburn, Virginia — a $427M site — donating 25 acres and $5M for housing under "Project Potomac Promise" as local data-center backlash grows. [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/news)
- **Pennsylvania enacted an AI advertising law:** the AI Deceptive Advertising Act casts a wide net over AI-generated ads, one of the first state laws of its kind. [AI News Hub](https://aienews.org/news.html)
- **A Berkeley math professor stopped grading homework for correctness** after AI tools made top marks meaningless — Fields Medalist Richard Borcherds now gives credit for completion, saying "there is no easy way to tell the difference between human and AI generated work." [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/10/25/us-news/uc-berkeley-professor-refuses-to-grade-students-homework-after-spotting-surge-in-ai-usage/)
