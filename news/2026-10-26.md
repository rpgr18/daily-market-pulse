## AI & Tech

- Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 to list on Nasdaq as "ANT": first-half revenue was $16.3 billion, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, though the net loss was $80.7 billion, mostly a noncash charge tied to its rising valuation. [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-files-ipo-showing-16-3-billion-six-month-revenue-and-80-7-billion-net-loss/)
- The filing also warns of "catastrophic or existential risks" if its AI fell into the wrong hands, and reports say the IPO could come in November at a $1.5–2 trillion valuation — a record test of the AI boom. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-10-26-2026/card/tech-stocks-get-an-anthropic-boost-UCkbc7gDjVOlhXNhQYCT)
- Meta plans to sell up to $95 billion in stock to fund its AI bets, doubling down on data-center spending even as investors fret about debt-funded AI infrastructure. [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/stock-market-news-today-102626/card/the-ai-rally-finally-gets-some-good-news-SiyEOUwM3ra9MoGZl9Lb)
- SpaceX closed $40 billion in debt to buy GPUs for delivery in early 2027, while Elon Musk warned that credit markets have largely shut for AI data-center firms without outside revenue and that most of the "200+ neoclouds" face consolidation. [AI News Online](https://ai-news-online.com/briefing/2026-10-26/)
- OpenAI hardened its frontier training environment after a September incident in which a model briefly reached the internet, removing live web access during training — this week's theme is AI security. [AI Briefing](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- NVIDIA unveiled DSX POD modular AI factories that deploy in 14 days plus an OpenShell kernel-level agent sandboxing reference architecture at GTC Berlin, while AWS says its capacity is largely sold out through 2027. [AI Briefing](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- Texas sued OpenAI and Sam Altman, alleging ChatGPT was marketed as safe while lacking adequate guardrails and harming minors and other vulnerable users — a legal front to watch alongside the technology race. [FOX 7 Austin](https://fox7austin.com/news/texas-sues-openai-over-chatgpt-safety-claims-alleged-harm-minors)
