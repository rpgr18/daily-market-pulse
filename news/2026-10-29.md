# News digest — October 29, 2026

Tech led a sell-off after Alphabet's earnings revived worries about whether AI spending is paying off fast enough, even as the Fed held rates steady the day before.

## Markets

- Tech led stocks lower: the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.4% to 26,185, the S&P 500 fell 0.3% to 7,720, and the Dow fell 0.3%, with the 10-year Treasury yield easing to around 4.94–4.95%. — [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10292026-12200056)
- Alphabet was the trigger: shares fell about 12% after Q3 revenue of $127.6 billion missed expectations, free cash flow turned negative by $9.1 billion, and capex hit $51.4 billion — a roughly $500 billion one-day market-cap loss. — [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-sp500-nasdaq-inflation-data-alphabet-earnings-microsoft/)
- Microsoft was the contrast: Azure revenue grew 48% to $33.17 billion, disclosed for the first time, with guidance for 49–50% growth next quarter — a sign AI spending can still convert to cloud revenue. — [AI Briefing](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- The Fed held rates at 3.75%–4.00% in a 10–2 vote on Wednesday, while September PCE came in slightly cooler than expected and Q3 GDP beat forecasts — yet traders now see about an 80% chance of a December hike. — [Saxo Morning Brew](https://www.home.saxo/en-ch/content/articles/morning-brew/morning-brew-october-29-2026-29102026)
- ServiceNow tumbled nearly 25% despite Q3 revenue rising 20% year over year to $4.1 billion — a reminder that growth alone is no longer enough when valuations and margins are under scrutiny. — [XTB](https://www.xtb.com/cy/market-analysis/news-and-research/daily-summary-bloody-thursday-on-wall-street-nasdaq-leads-the-sell-off)
- Caterpillar fell more than 9% and weighed on the Dow, while small-cap Russell 2000 bucked the weakness, gaining about 1%. — [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10292026-12200056)
- ADT surged 33% to $9.88 after agreeing to be acquired for $10.00 per share in cash, on volume roughly 15x its three-month average. — [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/29/stock-market-today-oct-29-adt-surges-33-on-usd10-per-share-all-cash-acquisition-deal/)
- Norwegian Cruise Line jumped 13.5% after beating Q3 estimates and raising full-year guidance, lifting Royal Caribbean and Carnival with it. — [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/29/stock-market-today-oct-29-norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-beats-q3-estimates-raises-full-year-guidance/)
