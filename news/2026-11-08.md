# News digest — Sunday, November 8, 2026

## Markets

- **Nasdaq enters correction as AI stocks crater.** The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.5% on Thursday — its worst day since April 2025 — closing more than 10% below its October 6 record, while the Nasdaq 100 dropped 6.4% (worst since March 2020) and the semiconductor index sank 12.3%. Investors are questioning the scale, financing, and returns of AI infrastructure spending. [Invest News Week](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/invest-news-week-newsletter-10-nasdaq-enters-correction-willem-tait-2wsge)
- **Thursday was Wall Street's worst day since April 2025.** The S&P 500 fell 4.36%, the Dow lost 1,833 points (3.4%), and the rout erased nearly $3 trillion in S&P 500 market value — driven less by earnings than by fears that a Democratic-controlled Congress could slow the AI data-center buildout. [The Retest](https://theretest.com/brief/2026-11-06-morning)
- **Friday brought a partial rebound, but the week still ended down.** The Nasdaq gained 1.1% Friday to 24,579 as chips bounced (AMD +11%, Micron +5%), yet the index lost 3.4% for the week — its fourth straight weekly decline — with the S&P down 2.7%. [BTIN](https://www.btin.co.in/nasdaq-rebounds-weekly-loss-ai-selloff-november-6-122274)
- **Jobs blowout pushes Fed hike odds to ~80%.** The US added 165,000 jobs in October, far above the 65,000 expected, with unemployment dipping to 4.1% and prior months revised up 84,000. Traders now price roughly an 80% chance of a Fed rate increase in December. [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:847a407fd094b:0-nasdaq-100-has-worst-day-since-2020-jobs-beat-keeps-fed-hike-alive-this-week-on-wall-street/)
- **Oil's worst week since 2022.** Brent crude fell about 17% on the week to settle at $98.32 a barrel, down from $117.64, as the geopolitical war premium unwound. [Invest News Week](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/invest-news-week-newsletter-10-nasdaq-enters-correction-willem-tait-2wsge)
- **Bank of England hikes for the first time since 2023.** The BoE raised its benchmark rate from 3.75% to 4.00%, a move that adds to the global rates pressure as the Fed leans hawkish. [Invest News Week](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/invest-news-week-newsletter-10-nasdaq-enters-correction-willem-tait-2wsge)
- **India rallies against the global selloff.** The Nifty 50 gained 8.6% and the Sensex 8.8% on the week — their best since February 2021 — as foreign investors bought $1.6 billion of Indian shares on Thursday alone. Sunday's special Diwali Muhurat session opened higher. [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/muhurat-trading-2026-sensex-rises-270-points-nifty-opens-at-25-070-tata-steel-hindalco-ongc-lead-gainers/article-203169/)

[Silicon Noon](https://siliconnoon.com/today-in-ai-november-8-2026/)

[Silicon Noon](https://siliconnoon.com/today-in-ai-november-8-2026/)

[The AI Daily Grind](https://aidailygrind.com/)

[The AI Daily Grind](https://aidailygrind.com/)
