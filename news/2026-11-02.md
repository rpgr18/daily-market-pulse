## Markets

- Stocks closed mixed as the early rally faded and rotation out of tech continued: the Dow rose 0.41% to 51,983.98, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.51% to 7,541.40 and the Nasdaq fell 1.07% to 25,164.32. [Source](https://www.sharecast.com/news/market-report-us-close/wall-street-nyse-dow-jones-nasdaq-stocks-equities--23989308.html)
- Oil tumbled on hopes for a U.S.–Iran diplomatic deal: Brent fell 8.23% to $104.09 a barrel and WTI dropped 7.78% to $96.02, easing energy costs but dragging energy stocks lower. [Source](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/02/stock-market-midday-nov-2-stocks-rally-as-oil-prices-tumble-on-diplomatic-hopes/?source=iedfolrf0000001)
- Friday's selloff set the tone: the Nasdaq fell 2.9% on Friday, its worst single-day drop in nearly five months, as AI names sold off after Amazon's earnings miss, with the VIX jumping to 19.42. [Source](https://WWW.ZACKS.COM/stock/news/3020468/stock-market-news-for-nov-2-2026)
- Apple reported after the bell — its first earnings under new CEO John Ternus — with September-quarter revenue up 12% and narrowly ahead of expectations, but shares slipped about 3% postmarket on margin and services worries; Palantir also reported after the close. [Source](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-11-02-2026)
- Oracle fell about 6.2% after unveiling a $20 billion fundraising ($10 billion in stock plus $10 billion in bonds) to finance AI infrastructure, a reminder that AI buildout spending is now being funded in size — while Amazon rallied more than 4% as investors reassessed its record cloud growth. [Source](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/02/stock-market-midday-nov-2-stocks-rally-as-oil-prices-tumble-on-diplomatic-hopes/?source=iedfolrf0000001)
- Intel's pain continued after its 28.6% plunge on Friday on a weak forecast despite beating estimates, with AMD down 10.9% and Nvidia down 4.2% on Friday as well — the chip selloff is still the market's main drag. [Source](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-sp500-nasdaq-oil-prices-apple-stock-apple-earnings/)
- Crypto steadied after last week's record Binance hack: Bitcoin held near $78,000 as Binance said it had covered about 90% of the affected funds and restored withdrawals. [Source](https://www.investopedia.com/5-things-to-know-stock-market-november-2-2026-12206140)
- Week ahead: Tuesday's midterm elections, Tuesday's JOLTS report, Wednesday's Treasury refunding announcement, and Friday's October jobs report will decide whether this tech rotation is a pause or a deeper reset. [Source](https://www.tradingview.com/news/prnewswire:da69e8690e235:0-u-s-index-futures-turn-to-jobs-data-and-treasury-s-refunding-after-friday-s-3-46-nasdaq-100-drop/)

## AI & Tech

- Marvell announced a $115 billion all-stock deal to acquire Lumentum, one of the biggest tech deals ever, betting big on AI connectivity — though both stocks fell sharply on Monday (Marvell -12.7%, Lumentum -18.6%) as investors questioned the price. [Source](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-futures-ai-trade-apple-earnings-aapl-stock-palantir-earnings-midterm-elections/)
- Anthropic filed for what could be the biggest IPO ever, planning to list on Nasdaq as 'ANT' and disclosing roughly $16–17 billion in revenue at a sharply higher run rate, alongside large losses — the clearest test yet of public-market appetite for pure-play AI labs. [Source](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- Meta plans to raise $35 billion through a stock offering to fund its AI ambitions, per SEC documents released Monday — another hyperscaler tapping markets to pay for the AI buildout. [Source](https://techxplore.com/machine-learning-ai-news/sort/date/6h/)
- Alibaba's Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for Apple users in mainland China, starting with the iOS 27.3 developer beta on November 4 — a notable local-model win for Apple in China. [Source](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- Google's Gemini 4 Carbon topped the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59, but its launch is gated to vetted cyber defenders, drawing backlash over restricted access to a frontier model. [Source](https://aifans.fan/news/)
- OpenAI launched its Grid Protectors Program to work with utilities so future AI compute campuses support grid reliability rather than raising consumer electricity costs, as it rebrands data centers as 'AI compute campuses'. [Source](https://aifans.fan/news/)
- Elon Musk throttled Grok Bot limits, citing 'unsustainably high' serving costs versus revenue — a sign that even xAI is feeling the economics of running large models at scale. [Source](http://m.techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/)
