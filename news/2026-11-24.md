# News digest — Tuesday, November 24, 2026

## Markets

- **Monday's rally fizzles — AI stocks lead the retreat.** Nvidia fell 5.9% a day after its record 31.5% surge on Jensen Huang's reinstatement, and Anthropic slid 6.5% to below its $83 IPO price. The S&P 500 fell 0.5% to 7,201 for its eighth loss in nine days, the Nasdaq dropped 1.5%, but the Dow added 423 points (+0.9%) as money rotated into non-tech names. [AP](https://connect.netscape.com/news/story/0001/20261124/feec0e25b9994b1c86b16329cf770192)
- **Consumer confidence jumps to a near two-year high.** The Conference Board index rose to 103.3 in November from 91.9, far above the 92.5 forecast — its highest level since January 2025. [Benzinga via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:10db8656d094b:0-anthropic-drops-below-ipo-prices-nvidia-sinks-7-after-record-rally-stock-market-today/)
- **Sanofi to buy Insmed for $41 billion.** Insmed jumped 14% on the $175-a-share deal, lifting health care to the day's top sector as investors rotated out of chips. [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/stock-movers-0853c431)
- **Retailers rally on earnings.** Dollar Tree jumped 11% after a strong quarter, Kohl's rose 19% on its first comparable-store sales growth in more than four years, and Abercrombie & Fitch soared 20%. [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/stock-movers-0853c431)
- **Chip earnings disappoint.** Analog Devices tumbled 17% — the S&P 500's worst performer — Hewlett Packard Enterprise plunged 16% despite beating estimates, and Micron fell 11% after BNP Paribas downgraded it and slashed its price target. [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/stock-movers-0853c431)
- **Tariff headlines add pressure.** Stocks accelerated lower after reports the US is preparing to raise tariffs on China and other countries the administration says are harming American companies by subsidizing domestic production. [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/8987701/weakness-in-tech-stocks-weighs-on-the-broader-market)
- **Oil steadies, gold and bitcoin slide.** WTI crude edged up to about $80.50 after three tankers were struck by unknown projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz; gold fell roughly 2.4% and bitcoin slipped below $60,000. [Benzinga via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:10db8656d094b:0-anthropic-drops-below-ipo-prices-nvidia-sinks-7-after-record-rally-stock-market-today/)

## AI & Tech

- **Anthropic puts Claude in charge of pull requests.** Claude Ship launched Nov. 23: Code Review is generally available, while PR Steward and Claude Approvals — which fix failing PRs and approve routine ones under a policy file — are in beta. [TopFive](https://topfive.ai/)
- **OpenAI rolls out GPT-6.2 to paid ChatGPT plans.** The chat-tuned model is coming to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise, with OpenAI claiming image understanding rose to 71.7% from 47.4% on its own tests. [TopFive](https://topfive.ai/)
- **Meta launches its Muse AI agent in the UK — and regulators push back.** UK regulators warned Meta over Muse's privacy and consumer protections as the agent connects to Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp; Meta has committed to voluntary monitoring and reporting with the privacy regulator. [The World of AI](https://theworldofai.org/ai-news/)
- **Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX over Grok deepfakes.** The states sued on Nov. 23, alleging Grok generated sexualized images of real people, including children, while the company claimed safeguards it did not have. [TopFive](https://topfive.ai/)
- **New York bans AI chatbot toys for five years.** Gov. Kathy Hochul signed the bill on Nov. 24, establishing a five-year pause on AI chatbot toys in the state. [The World of AI](https://theworldofai.org/ai-news/)
- **Trump threatens a "tremendous tax" on overseas data centers.** The president said US companies building data centers abroad will face punishing taxes and penalties, framing it as keeping America ahead of China in AI. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai_ml)
- **US blacklists Megaspeed hours after Huang's return.** The export-control action landed the same day Nvidia's CEO was reinstated — a reminder the legal and China-chip overhang hasn't lifted. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai_ml)
