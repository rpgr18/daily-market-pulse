([wsnext](https://wsnext.com/997d0d4-Artificial-Intelligence-Corporate-Governance/))

([NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/10/10/tech/new-ai-model-griffin-tricks-48-of-people-into-thinking-its-human/))

The cloud is no longer where AI is won — on-device inference is. ([SkimNews](http://skimnews.com/tech))
