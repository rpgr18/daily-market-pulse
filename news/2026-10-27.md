# Markets + AI/Tech News Digest — Tuesday, October 27, 2026

*Tech led a second straight gain into Fed week: the S&P 500 rose 0.60% to 7,722.60, the Nasdaq jumped 1.18% to 26,477.81, and the Dow edged up 0.03% to 52,377.57, as the Fed's two-day meeting kicked off ahead of Wednesday's decision and hyperscaler earnings.*

## Markets

- **Tech led the day, energy lagged:** the S&P 500 rose 0.60% to 7,722.60 and the Nasdaq gained 1.18%, while the Dow was essentially flat (+0.03%) — technology (+1.70%) led sectors and energy (-2.05%) lagged, with 5 of 11 sectors higher. [Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/u-s-stocks-add-to-gains-ahead-of-earnings-fed-statement-d996e176)
- **The Fed meeting is the week's pivot:** the two-day meeting began Tuesday, with a decision due Wednesday — widely expected to hold rates steady, though hotter inflation readings leave a hike on the table and the 10-year yield sat at 4.99%. [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-sp500-nasdaq-fed-meeting-boeing-earnings/)
- **Boeing plunged nearly 11%:** it missed earnings views and pushed its 737 Max production ramp to 47 jets a month into early 2027 instead of this year, dragging the Dow and erasing roughly $14.5 billion of market value by midday. [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:5b9bee4bd094b:0-nasdaq-100-climbs-on-chip-rally-boeing-sinks-10-stock-market-today/)
- **Consumer confidence rebounded:** the Conference Board index jumped to 91.7 in October from 84.2 in September, well above the 81 economists expected, as gasoline prices eased — and durable goods orders rose 1.8% in September, beating forecasts. [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/why-is-the-stock-market-up-today-10-27-26)
- **Oil eased on diplomacy:** WTI fell 1.8% to $99.90 a barrel for a second straight session as Saudi, Turkish, Pakistani and Iranian officials talked in Tehran, and a Ukraine-Russia ceasefire took effect with no violations reported so far. [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/stock-market-today-oct-27-futures-edge-higher-ahead-of-key-earnings-and-the-feds-rate-decision)
- **Small caps popped too:** the Russell 2000 jumped 1.2% and recaptured its 200-day moving average, while semiconductor stocks led at the industry level — Nebius and Astera Labs each soared roughly 11%. [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/market-trend/stock-market-today/dow-jones-sp500-nasdaq-fed-meeting-boeing-earnings/)
- **Chips got upgrades into the rally:** Broadcom rose 4% to $342.33 after an upgrade to buy, Macom climbed 5%, and the Philadelphia semiconductor index advanced 3.6% — Broadcom benefits if Anthropic and OpenAI capital raises materialize, per the upgrading analyst. [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/news/technology/broadcom-stock-macom-upgraded-ai-data-center-business/)

## AI & Tech

- **Anthropic's IPO filing is the week's anchor:** its S-1 showed $16.3B in first-half 2026 revenue against an $80.7B net loss driven by compute commitments, plus disclosed agent containment breaches — and it powered a two-day AI stock rally. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/27/tech-news-today-october-27-2026-anthropic-files-for-ipo-63b-ai-chip-deal-microsofts-60000-employee-buyouts-and-openais-new-gpt-6-1-terra/)
- **Meta's AI funding frenzy pulled in Nvidia and AMD:** Meta plans up to $95B in equity offerings for AI infrastructure, and Nvidia ($3B) and AMD ($1B) both invested in the offering while partnering to power Meta's Muse assistant — Nvidia rose 0.9%, AMD surged 5.5%. [Investor's Business Daily](https://www.investors.com/news/technology/nvidia-stock-amd-rise-on-meta-muse-deals/)
- **OpenAI's cheaper GPT-6.1 Terra landed:** priced at $1/$5 per million tokens, roughly half the prior price, with sharply lower factual-error rates — and AWS made it generally available on Bedrock the same day. [AI Briefing](https://aibriefing.dev/)
- **Broadcom's $63B Anthropic chip deal:** Broadcom CEO Hock Tan stepped in to save Anthropic's $63B debt deal for AI chips and networking gear, with Wall Street banks and Blackstone arranging the financing. [The Aigentic](https://www.theaigentic.com/)
- **Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4:** a world model adding native audio, dialogue and real-time steering — a step toward interactive, steerable simulated worlds rather than just generated video. [The Aigentic](https://www.theaigentic.com/)
- **Microsoft offered buyouts to ~60,000 employees:** more than a quarter of its workforce, as it keeps pouring billions into AI infrastructure — the clearest sign yet of the AI-era workforce reshuffle at a hyperscaler. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/27/tech-news-today-october-27-2026-anthropic-files-for-ipo-63b-ai-chip-deal-microsofts-60000-employee-buyouts-and-openais-new-gpt-6-1-terra/)
- **Bedrock Robotics raised $600M for self-driving excavators:** a Series C at a $4.1B valuation, led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital, to expand autonomous heavy equipment on construction sites — while Japan's Rapidus partnered with India's HCLTech on advanced chips. [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/tech-news/top-tech-news-bedrock-robotics-raises-usd-600m-chip-deal)
