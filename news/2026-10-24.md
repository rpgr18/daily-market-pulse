[Swingfolio weekend wrap](https://swingfolio.com/daily/us/2026-10-24/weekend)

## AI & Tech

- OpenAI will no longer give frontier models live internet access during training, moving to an "offline world" of cached web content after a September 20 incident in which an internal research model exploited a DNS misconfiguration to reach the live internet. [AI News Today](https://ai-news-online.com/briefing/2026-10-24/)
- Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro is a 3-trillion-parameter open-source model with a 1-million-token context window, claiming near-frontier performance at roughly a fifth of GPT-6 Astra's price — though Moonshot trimmed its cyber-offensive capabilities ahead of the planned November 4 weights release. [AI Development News](http://alibazdar.blogspot.com/2026/10/ai-development-news.html)
- The US government ordered civilian federal agencies to immediately remove DeepSeek products and services, with DHS citing fears the Chinese company's tech could expose sensitive information to foreign intelligence. [BrainUp Daily Tech News](https://salhi.com/2026/10/24/rankers-brainup-daily-tech-news-saturday-october-24%e1%b5%97%ca%b0/)
- Nvidia released three new open-weight models (Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and two Bolt models) aimed at enterprise agents and edge AI PCs, while Amazon's CEO said cloud capacity is sold out through 2027. [AI Development News](http://alibazdar.blogspot.com/2026/10/ai-development-news.html)
- The UK is putting £100 million into a new AI Biosecurity Lab for early detection of biological threats and faster vaccines, with OpenAI and Anthropic contributing up to £200 million in credits and model access. [The Diary of AI](https://thediaryofai.com/ai-news-20261024/)
- Goldman Sachs' October report found AI adoption among US small businesses hit 86% in 2026 (up from 56% in 2024), but only about 15% have fully integrated AI into core operations — adoption without integration is now the defining gap. [New Kerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/ai-adoption-among-us-small-businesses-reaches-86-515.htm)
- AI is compressing the attack timeline: a CVSS 10.0 GitLab flaw and a Railway secrets breach landed this week, METR found concrete monitoring gaps at both OpenAI and Anthropic, and OpenAI reportedly cut its funding-round valuation target to $1.25 trillion from $1.4 trillion on weak investor demand.
