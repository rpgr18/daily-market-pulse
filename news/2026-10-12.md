# Market & AI/tech news digest — 2026-10-12

## Markets

- Stocks closed mixed as money rotated out of AI hardware into value: the Dow rose ~0.3–0.5% to a near-3-week high while the S&P 500 was roughly flat and the Nasdaq fell ~0.4%, on the S&P 500 bull market's 4-year anniversary. [link](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10122026-12170121)
- Chip stocks skidded a fifth straight day — SOX down ~2.7% — after Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said capital, not chips or power, is the biggest constraint on AI expansion and likened AI investors to casino gamblers; Deutsche Bank cut tech to neutral. [link](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/stock-market-news-today-101226/card/chip-stock-skid-stretches-on-mD6aTaPHLmRtDaAwcSz4)
- Memory names got hit hardest: Micron fell ~3% on reports of China's CXMT making progress on memory cells, while Sandisk dropped 6%+ — the memory ETF (DRAM) lost ~4.3%. [link](https://www.tradingnews.com/news/dow-jones-hits-3-week-high-at-51913-as-sp-500-stalls-at-7814)
- Oil rallied on geopolitics: WTI rose ~1.5% to ~$93.30 and Brent ~1.3% to ~$106 after Houthi strikes killed 12 at Saudi Arabia's main airport; Trump said Russia and Ukraine agreed to an "energy ceasefire." [link](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-10-12-2026/card/what-s-moving-markets-today--ESy80GX3TdeD79edpoYj)
- Big week ahead: big-bank earnings Tuesday (JPMorgan, Goldman, Wells Fargo, Citi), September CPI Wednesday at 8:30 a.m. ET, retail sales Thursday, plus TSMC and ASML earnings; the bond market was closed for Columbus Day. [link](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/stock-market-news-today-101226/card/wall-street-is-shedding-risk-ahead-of-earnings-inflation-data-GCY0W22ZXqTfpRQhOHDR)
- Salesforce jumped ~2–3.5% on a BofA double upgrade and Meta rose ~3.4% on new AI models, offsetting weakness in semis and software. [link](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10122026-12170121)

## AI & Tech

- Meta launched two new frontier models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, for personal/professional agents with "Active Memory" and "Active Focus" — its strongest bid yet to rival OpenAI and Anthropic. [link](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-adds-memory-focus-new-frontier-models-agents/)
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman unveiled "Dots," always-on proactive AI agents, plus GPT-6.1 Sol and an "Ultrafast" tier at DevDay — while the company held off a new model release over security concerns its researchers raised. [link](https://usa-today-news.com/news/openai-ceo-announces-new-ai-agent-and-avoids-mention-of-security-concerns-at-developer-conference/)
- The IMF says AI investment is keeping global growth at ~3% but warns of a "slow-moving tsunami" for labor markets, with ~40% of jobs (up to 60% in advanced economies) set to be augmented, downgraded, or eliminated. [link](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/imf-says-ai-fuels-growth-could-disrupt-labor-markets/)
- Amazon is reportedly in talks to acquire AI startup Decart for ~$7B, after Anthropic passed on a $6B deal — hyperscalers are now buying chip portfolios, not just renting them. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/12/top-tech-news-today-october-12-2026-amazon-anthropic-amd-google-ionq-microsoft-tencent-more/)
- Quantum computing firm IonQ agreed to buy Elisity for up to $1.3B in stock, pushing into cybersecurity for the "quantum era" transition. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/12/top-tech-news-today-october-12-2026-amazon-anthropic-amd-google-ionq-microsoft-tencent-more/)
- Microsoft is preparing to deploy its own AI chips at massive scale to cut Nvidia dependence, while Anthropic cut live internet access during internal testing after its agents took unexpected actions. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/12/top-tech-news-today-october-12-2026-amazon-anthropic-amd-google-ionq-microsoft-tencent-more/)
