# News digest — Thursday, October 22, 2026

Thursday was a rotation day: Meta's hunt for AI capital spooked the market into dumping AI-linked stocks and piling into Treasuries, sending the Nasdaq to its worst day since June and the 10-year yield below 5% for the first time in nearly a month. Meanwhile, AI-discovered security flaws set off a global patch scramble.

## Markets

- **AI selloff day:** Nasdaq −2.6% to 26,008 (worst day since June, ~$1T of market cap shed), S&P 500 −1.2% to 7,663, while the Dow edged up 11 points to 52,867 — a classic risk-off rotation out of tech and into everything else ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10222026-12187670)).
- **Meta −7%, $124B of value gone:** after raising 2026 capex guidance to $135–145B, warning it will "continue investing aggressively" next year, and disclosing ~$825B in future AI infrastructure commitments (up from ~$700B in July), investors punished the stock; Q3 free cash flow was −$2.1B, Meta's first outflow since 2012 ([Investors.com](https://www.investors.com/news/technology/meta-earnings-meta-stock-q3-2026-ai-fears/)).
- **Chips hammered:** the PHLX semiconductor index −6.7% and SOXX −6.5%; the Roundhill memory ETF −7% on Micron (−6.3%), SanDisk, Seagate and Western Digital; Nvidia −4.4%; AI-debt poster child CoreWeave −10%; Lam Research fell on soft guidance despite record revenue ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-10222026-12187670)).
- **Tesla −3.1%, SpaceX −9.1%:** Tesla beat on earnings but cut 2026 capex from >$25B to ~$20B, raising questions about AI/robotics pace; SpaceX gave back most of Tuesday's merger-chatter pop as Musk stayed quiet on a possible Tesla merger ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/stock-movers-aecffefc)).
- **Oil jumps, bonds rally:** Brent crude topped $112 (highest since May) and WTI neared $99 after fresh strikes on Saudi Arabia and reports of US-Israeli plans for renewed attacks on Iran; the 10-year yield plunged 19bp to 4.95% — its biggest one-day drop since March 2023 ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/stock-market-news-today-102226/card/treasury-yields-drop-as-s-p-500-and-nasdaq-futures-fall-fL9UgwmPq825iHXkZNnI)).
- **Earnings winners and losers:** P&G +5.6% on its fastest organic revenue growth in three years, Blackstone +8%, Spotify rallied (306M paying subs), Lockheed raised its outlook, QuantumScape +4.3% on a solid-state battery line for AI data centers; Moderna −18% paced biotech lower, and weekly jobless claims unexpectedly fell to 194k ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-trading-day-graphic-2026-10-22/)).
- **Global contagion:** South Korea −3.5%, China −2%, Japan −2.5%, Europe's biggest fall in three weeks; French 10-year yields topped 5% for the first time since 2002 as the French/German spread hit 175bp, widest since 2011; Bitcoin −4% to ~$81,300 and gold −2% to a two-month low ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-trading-day-graphic-2026-10-22/)).

## AI & Tech

- **PHP2Shell:** AI-assisted researchers at OpenAI and Wiz found critical PHP flaws threatening up to 70% of the world's websites — likened to Heartbleed; the NSA and Europe's cybersecurity agency urged immediate patching, Cloudflare logged millions of exploit attempts, and some sites went offline preemptively ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/21/tech/php2shell-vulnerability-ai-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).
- **Meta's hunt for AI capital:** signaling it may need to raise "hundreds of billions of dollars" starting as early as this autumn triggered a broad rotation — investors dumped AI-linked names and piled into Treasuries, home builders and beaten-down software ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/metas-hunt-for-ai-capital-strikes-a-raw-nerve-on-wall-street-e2de1601)).
- **Zuckerberg's tax shield:** Meta agreed to award Zuckerberg non-voting shares he could sell to cover a potential California billionaire wealth tax without diluting his control, after he told the company he was considering leaving the state ([The Times](https://www.thetimes.com/business/technology/article/meta-pay-zuckerberg-wealth-tax-vmbcb929c)).
- **Anthropic's agent push:** Anthropic is taking on Meta's Muse and OpenAI's dots with a new AI agent, days before its S-1 could land as early as Friday — potentially the largest IPO in history ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/5-things-to-know-before-the-stock-market-opens-on-thursday-october-22-2026-12187570)).
- **IPO watch:** Anthropic could file as soon as Friday, paving the way for what could be the largest IPO in history ([Summa Money](https://summamoney.com/investing/the-daily/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-futures-fall-as-traders-assess-tesla-meta-earnings/)).
- **Legal AI funding frenzy:** Legora raised $550M at an $8.8B valuation, riding the AI procurement boom ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/tech-news/tech-news-today-digital-twins-boost-chipmaking-legora-valued-at-usd-88b-uk-graduate-jobs-slip-walmart-sets-ai-shopping-rules)).
- **OpenAI's revenue reality check:** OpenAI's revenue is reportedly ~$20B below earlier projections, even as China's Manus raised $500M+ in its first round since splitting with Meta ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence)).
