# Markets + AI/Tech News Digest — Wednesday, October 28, 2026

*The Fed held rates steady and stocks edged higher into a split after-hours reaction from Microsoft and Alphabet: the S&P 500 rose 0.28% to 7,744, the Nasdaq gained 0.27% to 26,550, and the Dow added 0.19% to 52,475, as Oracle surged on a bold 2030 revenue target and chip stocks rallied on SK Hynix's record results.*

## Markets

- **Fed held rates at 3.75%–4.00% in a 10–2 vote:** the pause followed September's hike, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissenting in favor of a quarter-point increase — a split that keeps a December hike on the table rather than signalling an end to tightening. [Reuters via Cruisin' 929](https://cruisin929.com/2026/10/28/fomc-holds-rates-steady-markets-mostly-follow-suit/)
- **Stocks closed modestly higher:** the S&P 500 gained 0.28% to 7,744, the Nasdaq rose 0.27% to 26,550, and the Dow added 0.19% to 52,475, with energy leading sector gains while real estate and utilities retreated. [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/28/stock-market-today-oct-28-markets-edge-higher-as-fed-holds-rates-steady/)
- **Oracle surged 10.73% to $144.54:** its Investor Day guidance pointed to at least $225 billion in fiscal 2030 revenue, with investors now watching free-cash-flow progress and AI capex discipline — volume ran about 161% above its three-month average. [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/28/stock-market-today-oct-28-oracle-surges-on-fiscal-2030-revenue-guidance-of-usd225-billion/)
- **AI infrastructure movers led, guidance losers lagged:** Vertiv jumped almost 17% on bullish long-term AI infrastructure demand forecasts, Wingstop climbed on a better-than-expected quarter, and F5 plummeted over 20% on guidance and an analyst downgrade. [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/28/stock-market-today-oct-28-markets-edge-higher-as-fed-holds-rates-steady/)
- **Chips rallied on SK Hynix:** Micron jumped 4.8% to its highest in over two weeks and the Philadelphia chips index rose 0.9% after SK Hynix reported a more than sevenfold jump in quarterly profit on AI infrastructure demand, offsetting weakness in megacaps such as Tesla and Apple. [Kitco / Reuters](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-10-28/wall-st-treads-water-chips-rise-megacaps-fall-ahead-fed-big-tech)
- **After-hours split in Big Tech:** Microsoft rose about 6% and Alphabet fell about 10% in after-hours trading following their results, so Thursday's open will test whether cloud growth and AI spending plans justify the AI trade's valuations. [Reuters](https://q106fm.com/2026/10/28/trading-day-fed-pauses-big-tech-reports/)
- **Oil rebounded, yields steepened:** WTI crude rose about 3% and Brent about 1.5%, gold rose 0.10% to $4,068.14, and the 10-year Treasury yield rose to about 5.02% — with the yield curve steepening after Chair Warsh's press conference even as stocks held their gains. [Reuters](https://q106fm.com/2026/10/28/trading-day-fed-pauses-big-tech-reports/)

## AI & Tech

- **Google DeepMind launched Genie 4:** billed as the first real-time world model to generate audio natively — music, ambient sound and interactive dialogue — with scenes staying consistent for up to two minutes, targeting gaming and training simulations. [Elites Zone](https://elites.zone/en/top5/2026-10-28)
- **Anthropic urged courts, not kill switches, for risky AI:** in an October 15 letter to California Governor Gavin Newsom, Anthropic said government should be able to seek court orders blocking models that pose a "serious risk," while opposing blanket kill switches in favor of targeted interventions such as disabling an agent or a customer. [Reuters via 98.5 The River](https://985theriver.com/2026/10/28/anthropic-tells-california-governor-courts-should-be-able-to-block-risky-ai-models/)
- **Malaysia seized $12.1 million of AI chips:** customs seized about 49.5 million ringgit worth of advanced AI chips this month, including 30 server units found October 15 at Kuala Lumpur International Airport's free trade zone, falsely declared as electronic components and suspected of being routed onward in Asia. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/ai-chips-worth-121-million-seized-malaysia-airport-customs-authority-says-2026-10-28/)
- **AI capacity deals kept shifting alliances:** OpenAI is reportedly planning to purchase Google's Tensor Processing Units, while Nvidia and Oracle expanded their collaboration around Vera Rubin processors — a reminder that AI rivals are also becoming each other's infrastructure suppliers. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/28/tech-news-today-october-28-2026-google-openai-oracle-nvidia-scale-ai-sk-hynix-more/)
- **SK Hynix reported a record quarter:** revenue of approximately $75.5 billion and operating profit approaching $63 billion on relentless demand for advanced memory, with production effectively committed through 2029 — the fundamental driver behind Wednesday's chip rally. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/28/tech-news-today-october-28-2026-google-openai-oracle-nvidia-scale-ai-sk-hynix-more/)
- **AI moved into everyday work apps:** Apple is adding assistants to Pages, Keynote and Numbers, Oracle plans business workflows in ChatGPT, and Opera is previewing local browser AI — the week's theme is AI inside the tools people already use, not just new chatbots. [Precise Impact AI](https://www.preciseimpact.ai/briefing/ai-news-digest-2026-10-28)
- **Cloudflare disclosed a fixed cross-customer AI leak:** an isolation failure in its GLM-5.3 Workers AI service between August 27 and September 9 exposed data in 155 responses, all with prompts over 681,280 tokens, with no evidence of deliberate exploitation — a warning that AI hosting isolation matters as much as model accuracy. [Precise Impact AI](https://www.preciseimpact.ai/briefing/ai-news-digest-2026-10-28)
