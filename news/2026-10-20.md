# News digest — Tuesday, October 20, 2026

Blue chips carried the day as oil unwound its geopolitical scare: the Dow jumped 1.02% while the AI trade wobbled again and SpaceX delivered a big Q3 beat after the close.

## Markets

- **Dow does the heavy lifting:** DJIA +1.02% to 52,495.88, S&P 500 +0.28% to 7,712.57, Nasdaq flat (−0.02%) — money rotated out of tech into healthcare and blue chips as oil eased on hopes of revived US–Iran talks ([MarketsOnDeck recap](https://medium.com/@marketsondeck/marketsondeck-after-market-tape-tuesday-october-20-2026-c44eba0c832d)).
- **Oil whipsaw:** Brent spiked above $110 to a five-month high on Houthi attacks against Saudi infrastructure, then unwound ~2.5% to ~$106 after reports that mediators proposed restarting US–Iran talks ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/why-is-the-stock-market-up-today-10-20-26)).
- **AI trade stumbles again:** South Korean AI cloud firm Elice crashed ~30% on its Kosdaq debut, pressuring CoreWeave, Nebius and chip stocks; investors are getting pickier about AI debt-funded buildouts ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nasdaq-futures-dip-oil-prices-climb-earnings-loom-2026-10-20/)).
- **Earnings winners and losers:** 3M +5% on a raised forecast, Equifax +11.4% and Hasbro +5.6% on beats; GM −6.9% on a soft outlook despite a $20B buyback, GE Aerospace −5% despite beating estimates ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/5-things-to-know-before-the-stock-market-opens-on-tuesday-october-20-2026-12183821)).
- **SpaceX beats big:** Q3 revenue $14.6B (+161% YoY), net income $4.4B vs a $1.1B loss a year ago, adjusted EBITDA $8.8B — all well above Street expectations; shares up ~5% after hours. Netflix also reported Q3 after the close ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/20/ceo-elon-musk-just-gave-spacex-investors-great-news/)).
- **Rates cooperating:** the 10-year yield eased to ~5.07–5.11%, and odds of a Fed hike at the Oct 28 meeting fell to 14.9% from 18.8% a day earlier ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/why-is-the-stock-market-up-today-10-20-26)).

## AI & Tech

- **OpenAI races to lock $30B at a >$1.4T valuation** before Anthropic's IPO next month, courting sovereign wealth funds and long-onlys that Anthropic is also chasing ([PYMNTS/Reuters](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-racing-to-raise-30-billion-ahead-of-anthropic-listing/)).
- **DHS bans DeepSeek from federal civilian agencies** — the order, issued July 24 and published in the Federal Register today, excludes DeepSeek products from agency purchasing and mandates removal from agency IT systems over security concerns ([Tesla News Wire](https://teslanewswire.com/articles/dhs-bans-deepseek-federal-civilian-agencies-removal-order-oct-20-2026)).
- **DeepSeek open-sources DeepInfer**, its production inference stack for the V4.1-Flash model, claiming to show the community how it keeps inference costs and energy use low — the same day Washington banned its models from civilian agencies ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/deepseek-open-sources-inference-tech-to-lower-ai-costs/)).
- **Mastercard launches Agent Pay for Machines**, a tool built on the open x402 protocol so AI agents can pay for digital services with defined budgets and no separate accounts or API keys ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/mastercard-debuts-tool-to-help-ai-agents-purchase-digital-services/)).
- **ASI raises $325M at a $3B valuation** for AI simulation and monitoring software used to forecast disruptions in air traffic, power grids and military ops — backed by a $99M Army contract and a $875M FAA contract ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/ai-startup-asi-valued-3-billion-latest-funding-round-2026-10-20/)).
- **AI agents on both sides of security:** a hacker used agentic tools to breach 100+ company sites with little human help, while Microsoft rolled out enterprise controls to assess and stop risky agents ([The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-october-20-2026/)).
