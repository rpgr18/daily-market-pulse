# Market & Tech News Digest — Tuesday, October 13, 2026

## Markets

- Earnings season opened to a flat market: S&P 500 slipped 0.02% to 7,805, Dow lost 0.35% to 51,670, and Nasdaq fell 0.15% to 27,204 — mixed bank results and a memory-chip sell-off offset early gains. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/13/stock-market-today-oct-13-markets-edge-lower-as-earnings-season-kicks-off/))
- Bank earnings were a mixed bag: Goldman Sachs beat on a 72% jump in equities-trading revenue, while JPMorgan missed ($5.57 vs. $5.90 consensus) on $50.2B in revenue and Dimon warned rate hikes could hurt borrowers. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/13/stock-market-midday-oct-13-bank-earnings-and-chip-pressure-drives-mixed-trading/))
- Memory-chip rout deepened: Micron fell ~3.6% and Sandisk slumped on news of a Chinese DRAM architecture breakthrough, and Broadcom's CEO compared some AI investment activity to gambling. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/13/stock-market-today-oct-13-markets-edge-lower-as-earnings-season-kicks-off/))
- J&J dropped ~6% after its MedTech division missed sales estimates, Blackstone fell 4.18% on rising yields and price-target cuts, and Rocket Companies hit a 52-week low after JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley downgrades. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/13/stock-market-today-oct-13-rate-pressures-and-analyst-downgrades-weigh-on-rocket-companies/))
- India bucked the global gloom: Sensex surged 1,109 points (+1.54%) to 73,033 and Nifty rose 1.6% on an IT earnings rally led by HCL Technologies (+7.44%). ([The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-stock-market-highlights-13-october-2026/article71574859.ece))
- Asia was split: Nikkei fell 1.82% and South Korea's KOSPI tumbled ~6% on chip fears, while crude stayed elevated (~$104.55 Brent), gold held near $4,182, and the 10-year sat at 5.24% ahead of Wednesday's CPI. ([Armstrong Economics](https://www.armstrongeconomics.com/market-talk/market-talk-october-13-2026/))

## AI & Tech

- Character.AI filed for bankruptcy and agreed to sell assets to Disney for just $20M — a striking fall for one of the most-hyped generative-AI startups. ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/13/top-tech-news-today-october-13-2026-character-ai-bankruptcy-metas-muse-wonder-amd-ai-chips-apples-smart-home-push-and-cybersecurity-threats/))
- Meta launched Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its most advanced AI models yet, in a renewed push to compete at the frontier. ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/13/top-tech-news-today-october-13-2026-character-ai-bankruptcy-metas-muse-wonder-amd-ai-chips-apples-smart-home-push-and-cybersecurity-threats/))
- Apple's "Welcome Home" event unveiled the HomeView smart home hub (6-inch display, A18 chip, Siri AI, face recognition), plus a HomePod mini 2 and an A19-powered Apple TV 4K launching Oct. 30. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/apple-next-launch-is-today/) / [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/2026/10/13/apple-homeview-features-colors-price-and-availability/92230013007/))
- China's AI chip push reshaped markets: Nvidia's share of China's data-center AI accelerator market fell to 34% in H1 2026 (shipments down 44%) while Huawei hit 28%, and Tencent is raising $4.5B in bonds to fund AI investment. ([Paisa Kawach](https://paisakawach.com/news/china-ai-chip-push-nvidia-huawei-tencent-bonds-asian-tech-selloff-october-13-2026))
- Canada committed an extra $500M over five years to its Regional AI Initiative ($700M total), plus $40M for Atlantic Canada, to get Canadian AI into production. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/economic-development-southern-ontario/news/2026/10/government-of-canada-expands-support-to-adopt-and-commercialize-artificial-intelligence.html))
- Ramp is productizing Glass, the in-house AI tool it built after burning $1.5M in AI tokens in a single week — pitched as how to become an AI-native company rather than just handing employees models. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/ramp-1-million-week-spending-leads-glass-ai-tool/))
- Infrastructure and security: Alibaba unveiled QwenGyre for extreme-long-horizon coding agents, Microsoft rolled out Maia 300 inference silicon, and Anthropic warned future autonomous AI could spread across poorly secured smart-home devices. ([DDS Boston](https://ddsboston.com/blogs/vibe-code-academy/ai-news-for-vibe-coders-daily-2026-10-13) / [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/13/top-tech-news-today-october-13-2026-character-ai-bankruptcy-metas-muse-wonder-amd-ai-chips-apples-smart-home-push-and-cybersecurity-threats/))
